## Why I started it

Having gone through the experience myself as an international student in Russia — from Voronezh to Moscow — I saw firsthand how little accessible, practical information exists in Arabic for students figuring out visas, university admissions, housing, and everyday life here. Union Arab RF fills that gap.

## Beyond the channel

This work connects directly to what I do professionally — I work in the **International Marketing and Recruitment Unit** at **[HSE University](https://www.hse.ru/staff/hsamran)**, where I help international students navigate the same journey at scale. Running Union Arab RF keeps me close to the community on a personal level, outside of my day job.
