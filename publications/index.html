    How to Choose a Russian University: Intelligent System Support for International Students
    M.A.M. Muaamar, L.V. Kruglova, H. Samran, and 1 more author
    In Digitalization of Management in Organizational Systems. Yekaterinburg, Russia. ISBN 978-5-7996-4114-6 , 2025
    Abs HTML PDF

    With the increasing number of international applicants interested in obtaining higher education in the Russian Federation, this paper proposes a hybrid recommendation system combining collaborative and content-based filtering, semantic text analysis (Sentence-BERT), and Explainable AI to help international students choose suitable Russian universities and programs.
